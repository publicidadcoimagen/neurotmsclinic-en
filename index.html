A professional evaluation determines treatment suitability, the protocol and follow-up. Each person has different needs.

YOUR QUESTIONS ANSWERED

Before you take the first step.

How do I know if I am a candidate?

Eligibility is determined through a medical evaluation. The clinician reviews your history, reasons for seeking care and possible contraindications.

How many sessions will I need?

The number and frequency of sessions depend on the indication and individual protocol. These will be explained during your consultation.

What might I feel during a session?

Before treatment begins, the team explains expected sensations, possible discomfort and procedural risks.

Can I continue taking my medication?

Share your medication list with the clinician. Any changes must be directed by your doctor; do not stop treatment on your own.

Can I request information through WhatsApp?

Yes. WhatsApp is used for general information and appointment coordination. Clinical evaluation and decisions take place during consultation.
